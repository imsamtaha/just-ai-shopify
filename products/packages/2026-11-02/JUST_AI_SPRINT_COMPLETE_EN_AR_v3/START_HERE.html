Use Visual brief to describe the image or short video. For our example, show a fictional slide before and after simplifying its heading. Mark it Ready only after checking facts, links and image rights. The planner does not publish posts; publish manually in your chosen channel.

STEP 05

Export a backup and test recovery

Use Export backup to save the JSON file after each session. Keep it with a dated copy of your plan. Import backup restores the saved plan and may replace your current entries. Use a disposable test copy for your first recovery check. Local browser saving alone is not a reliable backup.

صدّر نسخة JSON في كل جلسة واحفظها بتاريخ واضح. الاستيراد يعيد الخطة وقد يستبدل الحقول الحالية. جرّب الاستعادة على نسخة تجريبية. لا تعتمد على حفظ المتصفح وحده.

  • Export backup > keep dated JSON
  • Import only the intended backup
STEP 06

Record results and choose the next topic

After posting, record views, saves, replies and qualified inquiries in the metrics notes. Compare similar posts over the same observation period. Use the worked example for reference, not as proof of results. Repeat what was useful, and change one thing at a time in the next week.

Troubleshooting

  • Video does not play: open HOW_TO.mp4 in a local MP4 player or read the transcript.
  • File will not edit: make a local working copy and open it in an app that supports the format.
  • Arabic appears reversed: use an editor with right-to-left support.
  • Spreadsheet rates are blank: enter the required denominator; blank is not zero performance.
  • Planner data disappeared: restore your exported JSON backup. Browser storage can be cleared.
